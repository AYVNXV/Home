Policy.

Last updated: October 2026. This page explains what data is collected when you use this website or contact me for web development services.

01 — Information I Collect

When you use the contact form on this website, I may collect the following information:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The service you're interested in
  • Your project budget (if provided)
  • Your project description

This information is submitted voluntarily by you. The contact form drafts an email via Gmail — your data is sent directly through your own email account, not stored on a server by this website.

02 — How I Use Your Information

Any information you share through the contact form is used solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Discuss your project requirements
  • Provide a quote or proposal for web development work

I will never sell, share, or distribute your personal information to third parties.

03 — Cookies & Tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics tools, or any form of tracking. No personal data is collected automatically when you browse the site.

04 — Third-Party Services

This website uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — to load typefaces. Google may collect your IP address when fonts are requested. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages — the My Projects page shows live previews of my other websites, which are hosted on GitHub Pages. Loading a preview may send your IP address to GitHub. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Gmail — the contact form opens a Gmail compose window. Your data is handled according to Google's Privacy Policy.

05 — Data Security

Since no data is stored on any server by this website, there is minimal risk of data breach from the website itself. All communication happens through email, which is protected by your email provider's security measures.

06 — Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data I hold about you (from email correspondence)
  • Request deletion of any email communication between us
  • Withdraw consent for me to contact you at any time
